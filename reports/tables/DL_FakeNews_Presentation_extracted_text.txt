From Baseline to Multimodal AI
A text-only model can learn wording patterns,
sensational language, and topic cues. But it
cannot know whether the image supports the
headline. That is why the next stage adds
visual information and tests whether image-
text fusion can fix text-only errors.

Problem 3: Not Every Article Has a Valid Image
To make the evaluation more reliable, we manually inspected image–
title–label triples and removed around 500 visually misleading or
label-inconsistent samples from nearly 5,000 image-based records.
